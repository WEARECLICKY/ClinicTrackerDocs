## Overview

Impersonation allows a support admin to temporarily sign in as a specific clinic user to troubleshoot issues.

## How it works

1. From the Support Dashboard, find the user
2. Click **Impersonate**
3. A visible banner shows the target user and an End Impersonation button
4. The session auto-expires after a set time

## Ending impersonation

Click **End Impersonation** in the banner or click **Sign Out**. Your admin session is restored.

<Warning>Impersonation gives full access to the user's account. Follow safety guidelines strictly.</Warning>

<Check>Always get permission first — confirm with the clinic owner.</Check>

<Check>Only impersonate to troubleshoot — don't make changes unless asked.</Check>

<Check>End the session promptly once you've diagnosed the issue.</Check>
