## Overview

The Staff Management tab lets support admins add or remove staff from any organisation.

## Adding a staff member

1. Select the organisation
2. Click **Add Staff Member**
3. Enter email, name, and role
4. Click **Add**

## Removing a staff member

Find the staff member, click **Remove**, and confirm. The removal is immediate.

<Warning>Removing a staff member is immediate and cannot be undone from the support dashboard.</Warning>
