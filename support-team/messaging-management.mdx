## Overview

The Messaging Management tab lets support admins configure email and SMS settings for any organisation.

## What you can manage

From email address, email domain verification status, SMS from number, and SMS sender ID.

<Tip>If a clinic is in a rush and domain verification is pending, they can use a verified default sender.</Tip>
