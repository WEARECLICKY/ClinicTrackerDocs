## Overview

The Reports page provides analytics across your clinic's operations, filterable by custom date ranges.

## Available reports

- **Revenue** — by treatment type and staff member
- **Client metrics** — new clients, active clients, retention rate
- **Treatment statistics** — most frequently performed treatments
- **Staff performance** — treatments performed, revenue, commission earned

<Tip>Review your reports weekly to spot trends early.</Tip>
