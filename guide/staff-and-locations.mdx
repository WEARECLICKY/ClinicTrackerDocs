## Overview

The Staff & Locations page manages everyone who works at your clinic and the locations where you operate.

## Adding a staff member

1. Click **Invite Team Member**
2. Enter email and full name
3. Assign one or more roles (Owner, Practitioner, Front Desk, Accountant, Prescriber)
4. Click **Send Invite**

## Treatment qualifications

For practitioners, define which treatments they're qualified to perform. Only qualified practitioners appear in the booking dropdown.

## Prescriber status

Each profile includes prescriber status: Prescriber, Remote Prescriber, or Not a Prescriber.

## Locations
