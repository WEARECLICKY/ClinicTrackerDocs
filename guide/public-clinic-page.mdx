## Overview

Every ClinicTracker organisation gets a public-facing landing page at /clinic/:orgId showing your branding, contact details, opening hours, and links to online booking and gift cards.

## What's on the page

Clinic logo, name, tagline, contact details, opening hours, social media links, a Book Now button, and a Buy Gift Card button.

## Customisation

All content is controlled by your Settings. Changes appear immediately.

<Tip>Make sure your opening hours are always up to date, especially around holidays.</Tip>
