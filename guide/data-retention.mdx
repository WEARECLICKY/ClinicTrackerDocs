## Overview

The Data Retention page lets you configure retention policies for different categories of data to comply with GDPR.

## Retention policies

Each policy covers a data category (Clinical, Financial, Marketing, Consent, Photos, or All Data) with a retention period in years and an action (Anonymize, Delete, or Retain Indefinitely).

<Warning>These are suggested defaults only. Consult your regulatory body before finalising retention policies.</Warning>
