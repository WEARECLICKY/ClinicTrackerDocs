## Overview

The Clients page is your clinic's CRM. Every person who has contacted, visited, or been treated at your clinic appears here.

## Searching and filtering

Type a name, email, or phone number into the search bar. Use the status filter for Active, Inactive, or Prospective clients.

## Adding a new client

1. Click **Add Client**
2. Fill in personal details (name, date of birth, contact information)
3. Set consent preferences (treatment and marketing)
4. Click **Save**

## Risk flag indicators

A yellow warning badge shows how many active clinical risk flags a client has.

## Bulk import

Go to **Import** to upload a CSV file. The system detects duplicates by email address.
