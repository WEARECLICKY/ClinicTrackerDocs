## Overview

The Treatment Protocols page lets you define standard protocols for each treatment type.

## Creating a protocol

1. Click **Add Protocol**
2. Enter name, treatment type, and category
3. Add steps with title and detailed instructions
4. Fill in dosage guidelines and product recommendations
5. List contraindications and required qualifications
6. Set typical sessions and interval
7. Click **Save**

<Check>Consistency — Every practitioner performs treatments the same way.</Check>
