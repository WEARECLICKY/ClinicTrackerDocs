## Overview

The Invoices page manages all billing for your clinic.

## Invoice statuses

| Status | Colour | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Draft | Grey | Created but not sent |
| Sent | Blue | Sent to the client |
| Paid | Green | Fully paid |
| Partial | Amber | Partially paid |
| Overdue | Red | Past due date |
| Void | Grey | Cancelled |

## Creating an invoice

1. Click **Create Invoice**
2. Select the client
3. Add line items with description, quantity, and unit price
4. Save as Draft or mark as Sent

Invoices are also created automatically for online bookings.
