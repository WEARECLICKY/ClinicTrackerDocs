## Overview

The Dashboard is the first page you see when you sign in — a real-time snapshot of your clinic's day.

## Client statistics

Four stat cards: Total clients, Active clients, Prospective clients, and Risk-flagged clients.

## Today's appointments

A chronological list of every appointment scheduled for today with client name, treatment, time, and colour-coded status badge.

## To-do list

Track clinical and administrative tasks with priority levels (Low, Normal, High, Urgent). Overdue tasks are highlighted in red.

## Pending consent alerts

Consent forms awaiting a client's signature appear here.

## Inventory alerts

When stock items fall below their reorder threshold, they appear here.
