## Overview

The Pricing page defines all treatments and products with prices, durations, and categories.

## Adding a price item

1. Click **Add Price Item**
2. Enter treatment name and select a treatment type
3. Set price and duration
4. Choose a category
5. Toggle Active and Bookable Online
6. Click **Save**

<Tip>Keep your price list organised with clear category names.</Tip>
