## Overview

The Segments page lets you create groups of clients based on shared characteristics for targeted messaging.

## Creating a segment

1. Click **Create Segment**
2. Enter name and description
3. Define filter criteria (treatment history, status, tags, date ranges)
4. Toggle Active
5. Click **Save**

## Using segments for targeted messaging

Open a segment, click **Send Message to Segment**, choose channel, compose, and send.

<Tip>Create a Lapsed Clients segment for clients who haven't visited in 6+ months.</Tip>
