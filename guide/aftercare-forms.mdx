## Overview

The Aftercare Forms page contains a library of post-treatment aftercare advice documents.

## Smart treatment matching

When you log a treatment, the system automatically suggests the matching aftercare form. Print or email it directly from the treatment form.

## Preview and print

Click any form to preview with your clinic's branding. One-click printing produces a branded document.

## Send to client

Send an aftercare form directly to a client by email. The sent message appears in the client's Communications tab.

<Tip>Send the aftercare form by email even if you also give a printed copy.</Tip>
