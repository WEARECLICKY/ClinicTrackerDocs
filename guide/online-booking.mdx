## Overview

ClinicTracker includes a public-facing booking widget that lets clients book appointments online without needing to sign in.

## Booking settings

Configure in Online Booking: enable/disable, select available treatments, set time slots, booking windows, buffer times, and payment requirements (full payment or deposit).

## The public booking widget

Clients choose a treatment, pick a date and time, enter their details, and pay if required. The system respects practitioner availability and prevents double-booking.

## Automatic client creation

If the client's email matches an existing client, the booking is linked. Otherwise, a new client record is created automatically. An invoice is also created automatically.
