## Overview

The Point of Sale (POS) is your front-desk payment terminal. Browse your catalogue, build a cart, attach a client, and process card payments through Stripe.

## Building a sale

1. Click an item from the catalogue to add it to the cart
2. Adjust quantities
3. The running total updates automatically

## Processing a card payment

1. Click **Charge**
2. Enter card details in the Stripe form
3. Payment processes in GBP
4. Confirmation screen shows the amount paid

## Buy Now, Pay Later

Offer Klarna or Clearpay for spread payments. Configure in Settings.

<Tip>Make sure Stripe is in live mode (not test mode) before processing real payments.</Tip>
