## Overview

The Account & Billing page manages your ClinicTracker subscription.

## Subscription statuses

| Status | Colour | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Free Trial | Blue | On a free trial |
| Active | Green | Subscription is current |
| Past Due | Amber | A payment failed |
| Canceled | Grey | Cancelled, access until period ends |
| Expired | Red | Subscription has ended |

<Tip>Keep an eye on your trial end date and add a payment method before it expires.</Tip>
