## Overview

The Messaging page lets you send email and SMS messages to clients with templates and delivery tracking.

## Message statuses

| Status | Colour | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Pending | Grey | Queued for sending |
| Sent | Blue | Delivered to the provider |
| Delivered | Green | Confirmed delivered |
| Failed | Red | Delivery failed |
| Read | Teal | Recipient opened the message |

## Message templates

Save reusable templates for common communications.

## Organisation sender identity

Configure from email address, SMS from number, and SMS sender ID in Settings. Verify your email domain for reliable delivery.

<Tip>Always use a verified email domain for sending client communications.</Tip>
