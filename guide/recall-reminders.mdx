## Overview

The Recall Reminders system tracks when each client is due for a repeat treatment based on their treatment history and recall intervals.

## Recall statuses

| Status | Colour | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Pending | Blue | Reminder not yet sent |
| Overdue | Red | Past the due date |
| Sent | Teal | Reminder has been sent |
| Booked | Green | Client has booked a follow-up |
| Skipped | Grey | Manually skipped |

## Generating reminders

For overdue or pending recalls, select clients and send reminders by email or SMS.
