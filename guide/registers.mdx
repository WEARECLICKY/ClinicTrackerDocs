## Overview

The Registers page helps you maintain statutory registers required for aesthetic practice in the UK.

## Register types

JCCP, Save Face, CPSA, GMC, NMC, GDC, and Other.

## Register statuses

| Status | Colour | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Active | Green | Currently registered |
| Lapsed | Amber | Registration has lapsed |
| Suspended | Red | Registration suspended |
| Pending | Blue | Application in progress |
| Not Registered | Grey | Not registered |

<Tip>Keep your registers up to date and review before inspections.</Tip>
