## Overview

The Licences page tracks all professional licences required for your clinic and staff.

## Licence types

Laser/IPL, Skin Piercing, Electrolysis, Semi-Permanent Makeup, Mesotherapy, Premises, Other.

## Renewal statuses

| Status | Colour | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Current | Green | Licence is valid |
| Renewal Due | Amber | Approaching expiry |
| Renewal Submitted | Blue | Awaiting confirmation |
| Expired | Red | Licence has expired |
| Not Required | Grey | No licence needed |

<Tip>Review your licences monthly and start renewals early.</Tip>
