## Overview

The Treatment Log records every detail of a treatment session — device settings, products used, treatment area pinpoints, before/after photos, and outcome notes.

## Logging a new session

Click **Log Treatment** to open the form:

- **Client and date** — select the client and treatment date
- **Treatment type** — choose the type (Injectable, Laser, Skin, etc.)
- **Device and settings** — enter device name and configure settings
- **Products used** — record product name, batch number, quantity
- **Treatment area diagram** — pin exact sites on a body map (Front, Back, Face)
- **Outcome notes** — record how the treatment went
- **Photo upload** — upload before, after, or progress photos
- **Aftercare matching** — system suggests the matching aftercare form
- **Settings carry-over** — previous session settings can be carried over

<Tip>Always record batch numbers for products used — essential for traceability and regulatory compliance.</Tip>
