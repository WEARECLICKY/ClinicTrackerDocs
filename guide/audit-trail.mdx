## Overview

The Audit Trail is a comprehensive log of every create, update, and delete action in ClinicTracker. It supports CQC and GMC audit requirements.

## What's logged

Every data change is recorded with the action (Create, Update, Delete), entity type, the actor, timestamp, and metadata showing what fields changed.

<Check>Regulatory compliance — Demonstrates to CQC inspectors that you maintain a complete record.</Check>

<Check>Accountability — You can always trace who made a change and when.</Check>
