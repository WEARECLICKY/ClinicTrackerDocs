## Overview

The Reviews page helps you manage your clinic's online reputation.

## Sending review requests

After a treatment, click **Send Review Request**, select the client, choose the platform (Google, Trustpilot, or Custom), and enter your review URL.

## Review request statuses

Pending, Sent, Clicked, Reviewed, Dismissed.

<Tip>Send review requests within 24 hours of a treatment.</Tip>
