## Overview

The Consultations page lets you create detailed, structured consultation records capturing the client's goals, your assessment, a recommended treatment plan, and follow-up details.

## Creating a consultation

Click **New Consultation** to open the form:

- **Treatment interest and goals** — what the client wants and their aesthetic goals
- **Concerns and assessment** — specific concerns and your professional assessment
- **Recommended treatment plan** — multi-line plan with treatment, area, sessions, price per line. Estimated total calculates automatically.
- **Follow-up and outcome** — set a follow-up date and record the outcome
- **Status** — save as Draft or Signed

<Tip>Save as Draft while still working, then mark as Signed once completed.</Tip>
