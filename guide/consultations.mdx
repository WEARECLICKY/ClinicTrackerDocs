## Overview

The Consultations page lets you create detailed, structured consultation records capturing the client's goals, your assessment, a recommended treatment plan, and follow-up details.

## Creating a consultation

Click **New Consultation** to open the form: treatment interest and goals, concerns and assessment, recommended treatment plan (multi-line with treatment, area, sessions, price), follow-up and outcome, and status (Draft or Signed).

<Tip>Save as Draft while still working, then mark as Signed once completed.</Tip>
