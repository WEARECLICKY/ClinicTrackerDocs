## Overview

The Inventory page tracks all products and supplies used in your clinic.

## Adding a stock item

1. Click **Add Stock Item**
2. Enter product name, category, and stock type (Professional or Retail)
3. Set quantity, unit, and reorder threshold
4. Add supplier details, batch number, and expiry date
5. Click **Save**

## Stock adjustments

Choose transaction type (Stock In, Stock Out, Adjustment, Waste), enter quantity change, and add notes. Every adjustment is logged.

## Low stock alerts

When items fall below their reorder threshold, an alert appears on the Dashboard.

<Tip>Set reorder thresholds realistically based on supplier lead times.</Tip>
