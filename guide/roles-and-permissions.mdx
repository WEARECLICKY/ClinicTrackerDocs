## Available roles

ClinicTracker uses role-based access control. Each team member sees only the features relevant to their job.

### Owner — full access to everything
### Practitioner — clinical and scheduling features
### Front Desk — scheduling and point of sale
### Accountant — financial features
### Prescriber — POM prescription approval in addition to practitioner access

A single team member can hold multiple roles. Treatment qualifications control which treatments a practitioner can be assigned to in the booking system.
