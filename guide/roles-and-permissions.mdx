## Available roles

ClinicTracker uses role-based access control to make sure each team member sees only the features relevant to their job.

### Owner

Full access to everything: manage staff, configure all settings, view reports and billing, and access all clinical, scheduling, payment, and compliance features.

### Practitioner

Access to clinical and scheduling features: manage client records, record consultations and treatments, create consent forms and aftercare, upload photos, view calendar, create invoices.

### Front Desk

Access to scheduling and point of sale: manage calendar, add clients, process payments, create invoices, send messages, manage waitlist.

### Accountant

Access to financial features: view invoices and payments, view reports, manage pricing and client balances, view commissions, access accounting integrations.

### Prescriber

In addition to practitioner access: approve or decline POM prescriptions, sign prescriptions digitally, transmit to pharmacies, manage the POM workflow.

## Assigning multiple roles

A single team member can hold multiple roles. The platform combines the permissions of all assigned roles.

## Treatment qualifications

Practitioners can be assigned treatment qualifications controlling which treatments they can be assigned to in the booking system. Manage in Staff & Locations.
