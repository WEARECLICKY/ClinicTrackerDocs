## Overview

The Gift Cards page lets you create, track, and manage gift cards. Clients can purchase online through the public widget, or you can create them manually.

## Creating a gift card

1. Click **Create Gift Card**
2. Enter the initial balance
3. Optionally link to a client
4. Set an expiry date (optional)
5. Click **Save**

## Online gift card purchases

The public gift card widget lets anyone buy a gift card. The buyer chooses an amount, enters recipient details, and pays through Stripe.
