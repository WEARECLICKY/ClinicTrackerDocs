## Overview

The Staff Commissions page tracks commission rules and earnings for each practitioner.

## Commission rules

Each rule defines a practitioner, commission type (Percentage or Fixed Amount), rate, optional treatment or category link, effective dates, and active status.

## Commission earnings

Earnings are calculated automatically as treatments are performed and paid for. Each earning shows practitioner, rule name, base amount, commission amount, and status (Accrued, Paid, or Reversed).

<Tip>Set commission rules with clear effective dates to avoid ambiguity.</Tip>
