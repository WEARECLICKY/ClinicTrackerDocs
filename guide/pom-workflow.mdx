## Overview

The POM Workflow ensures every POM prescription follows the correct process — from request through prescriber approval to pharmacy transmission.

## Key regulatory requirements

- **Face-to-face assessment** — must be confirmed before prescribing
- **Prescriber verification** — only qualified prescribers can approve
- **Stock linkage** — each POM links to a stock item with batch number
- **Pharmacy directory** — managed in Settings
- **Audit trail** — every action is recorded

<Warning>POM prescriptions are subject to strict regulatory controls. Ensure all prescribers have verified status.</Warning>
