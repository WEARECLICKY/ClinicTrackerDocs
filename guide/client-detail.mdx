## Overview

The Client Detail page brings together everything about a single client into a tabbed interface.

## Tabs

- **Overview** — summary card with quick actions (book appointment, log treatment)
- **EMR** — unified timeline of consultations, treatments, photos, and consent forms
- **Medical History** — conditions, medications, allergies, GP details, emergency contact
- **Risk Flags** — clinical concerns with type, severity, and active status
- **Treatments** — complete treatment history
- **Appointments** — upcoming and past appointments
- **Consultations** — all consultation records
- **Communications** — full log of emails and SMS sent to the client
- **Photos** — before, after, and progress photos by date
- **Loyalty & Gifts** — gift cards and loyalty enrolments
- **GDPR & Data** — manage data rights (Access, Rectification, Erasure, Portability)
