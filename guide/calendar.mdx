## Overview

The Calendar is your week-view appointment grid from Monday to Sunday with 30-minute time slots from 8am to 6pm.

## Appointment status colours

| Status | Colour | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Scheduled | Blue | Booked and waiting |
| In Progress | Amber | Treatment under way |
| Completed | Green | Finished successfully |
| Cancelled | Grey | Called off |
| No Show | Red | Client did not attend |

## Booking an appointment

1. Click any empty time slot
2. Select the client and treatment
3. Choose the practitioner (only qualified ones appear)
4. Adjust time and duration if needed
5. Click **Save**

<Tip>Click an empty slot to book — you don't need a separate booking page.</Tip>
