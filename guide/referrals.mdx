## Overview

The Referrals page tracks how new clients find your clinic and rewards clients who refer friends.

## Referral statuses

| Status | Colour | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Pending | Blue | Referred person hasn't become a client |
| Converted | Amber | Referred person has become a client |
| Rewarded | Green | Referrer has received their reward |
| Expired | Grey | Referral expired |

## Creating a referral

1. Click **Add Referral**
2. Select the referring client
3. Enter the referred person's details
4. Set the reward type
5. Click **Save**

<Tip>Offer a double-sided reward to encourage more referrals.</Tip>
