## Overview

The Waitlist helps you manage clients who want earlier appointments. When a cancellation creates an open slot, find waitlisted clients who match.

## Waitlist statuses

| Status | Colour | Meaning |
|--------|--------|---------|
| Waiting | Blue | Actively waiting |
| Offered | Amber | An appointment has been offered |
| Booked | Green | Client has accepted |
| Expired | Grey | Entry has expired |
| Cancelled | Red | Entry was cancelled |

## Adding a client

1. Click **Add to Waitlist**
2. Select the client and treatment
3. Specify preferred dates and practitioner (optional)
4. Set priority
5. Click **Save**

<Tip>Review your waitlist whenever a cancellation comes in.</Tip>
