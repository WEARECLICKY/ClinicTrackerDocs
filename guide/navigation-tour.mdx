## The sidebar

The left sidebar is your main way to move around ClinicTracker. On desktop, it's always visible. On mobile and tablet, tap the menu icon to open it as a slide-in drawer.

### Pinned items

Four items are always at the top: **Dashboard**, **Calendar**, **Clients**, and **Point of Sale**.

### Collapsible groups

Below the pinned items, features are organised into expandable groups: Clinical, Scheduling, Payments & Retail, Communication & Growth, Compliance & Regulatory, and Admin.

### Support section

If you have the Support Admin role, an additional **Support Dashboard** link appears at the bottom of the sidebar.

## The header bar

Shows your clinic logo and organisation name, a support actions button, and your profile avatar for signing out.

## Toast notifications

When you perform actions like saving a client or processing a payment, a small notification appears briefly at the bottom of the screen.
