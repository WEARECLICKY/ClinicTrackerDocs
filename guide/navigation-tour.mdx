## The sidebar

The left sidebar is your main way to move around. On desktop, it's always visible. On mobile, tap the menu icon.

### Pinned items

Dashboard, Calendar, Clients, and Point of Sale are always at the top.

### Collapsible groups

Clinical, Scheduling, Payments & Retail, Communication & Growth, Compliance & Regulatory, and Admin.
