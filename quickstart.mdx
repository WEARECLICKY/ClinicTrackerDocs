## Step 1: Create your account

Visit the sign-in page and click **Sign up**. Enter your email, password, clinic name, and full name. You'll be signed in on a free trial.

## Step 2: Set up your clinic profile

Go to **Settings** to upload your clinic logo, set your brand tagline, add contact details, and configure opening hours.

## Step 3: Add your team

Go to **Staff & Locations** to invite team members by email with a role, assign treatment qualifications, and add clinic locations.

## Step 4: Set up your price list

Go to **Pricing** to add treatments with prices, durations, and categories. Toggle **Bookable online** for self-bookable treatments.

## Step 5: Connect your payment provider

Go to **Settings** to connect Stripe. You can also configure Klarna and Clearpay for Buy Now Pay Later.

## Step 6: Add your first client

Go to **Clients** and click **Add Client** to enter their personal details and consent preferences.

## Step 7: Book your first appointment

Go to **Calendar**, click an empty time slot, select the client and treatment, and save.
