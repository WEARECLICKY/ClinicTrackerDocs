## Step 1: Create your account

When you first visit ClinicTracker, you'll see the sign-in page. Click **Sign up** to create a new account.

You'll need to provide your email address, a secure password, your clinic name (this becomes your organisation), and your full name. After signing up, you'll be automatically signed in and taken to your dashboard on a free trial.

## Step 2: Set up your clinic profile

Go to **Settings** in the left sidebar to upload your clinic logo, set your brand tagline, add contact details, and configure social media links. Then set your opening hours.

## Step 3: Add your team

Go to **Staff & Locations** to invite team members by email with a role (Owner, Practitioner, Front Desk, Accountant, or Prescriber), assign treatment qualifications, and add clinic locations.

## Step 4: Set up your price list

Go to **Pricing** to add treatments with prices, durations, and categories. Toggle **Bookable online** for treatments clients can self-book.

## Step 5: Connect your payment provider

Go to **Settings** to connect Stripe by entering your publishable and secret keys. You can also configure Klarna and Clearpay for Buy Now Pay Later.

## Step 6: Add your first client

Go to **Clients** and click **Add Client** to enter their personal details and consent preferences.

## Step 7: Book your first appointment

Go to **Calendar**, click an empty time slot, select the client and treatment, and save.
