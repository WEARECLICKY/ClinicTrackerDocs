## Can't sign in

1. Double-check your email for typos
2. Check that Caps Lock is not on
3. Try resetting your password

## Password reset

1. Go to the sign-in page and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email
3. Check your inbox for a reset email
4. Click the link and enter a new password

<Warning>The password reset link expires after a short time.</Warning>

## Can't access a feature

This is usually a role-based access issue. Check your role by clicking your profile avatar.
