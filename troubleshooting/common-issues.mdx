## Stripe payments not working

1. Check Stripe is configured with both keys in Settings
2. Ensure you're in live mode
3. Re-validate the connection

## Clients not receiving emails

1. Check from-email address is set
2. Verify your email domain
3. Check client's email is correct
4. Ask client to check spam

## Online booking showing no slots

1. Check opening hours are configured
2. Check practitioner availability
3. Check treatment qualifications
4. Check minimum notice period

## How to report a new issue

Click the support actions button in the header and submit a support request.
