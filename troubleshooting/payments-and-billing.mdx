## Card payment fails at the POS

1. Verify both Stripe keys are entered
2. Ensure you're in live mode
3. Click Validate
4. Try a different card

## BNPL options not appearing

1. Check BNPL providers are configured in Settings
2. Verify they're in live mode
3. Validate the connections

## Online payment fails during booking

1. Check Stripe is in live mode
2. Check the treatment price is greater than zero
3. Check your Stripe account is active

## Subscription shows Past Due

A recent payment failed. Go to Account & Billing and update your payment method.
